About us

We're building the calendar content teams actually open every day

Campaign Span brings planning, production and results into one place, so social, email, production, brand, sales and marketing teams can work from the same plan.

Why Campaign Span exists

Most content teams run on a patchwork: a calendar in one tool, briefs in documents, shoot schedules in spreadsheets, results in a dozen dashboards, and updates scattered across email and chat. Deadlines slip because the right people find out too late, and reporting turns into a manual job every month.

Campaign Span replaces the patchwork with one workspace. Every piece of content has an owner, every change reaches the people it affects, and every post comes back as data that shapes what you plan next.

What we believe

Easy beats powerful

A tool only works if people use it every day. We design for the daily check-in, not the quarterly planning session.

Every post has an owner

That's why tagging a person or department is required on every calendar entry. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Data should suggest, not just report

Charts are a start. Campaign Span points out the gaps and tells you what to try next.

Your data is yours

We collect only what the service needs, never sell it, and make it easy to export. See our privacy policy.

Where we are
