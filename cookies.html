1. What cookies and browser storage are

Cookies are small text files a website asks your browser to save. Browser storage (such as "local storage") works in a similar way. Both let a site remember something between visits. They can be used for essential features, preferences, analytics or advertising.

2. What our website stores

Our website sets no cookies. It uses your browser's local storage only for the preferences below. This information stays on your device and is never sent to us.

NamePurposeTypeKept until
cs-themeRemembers whether you chose light or dark modePreferenceYou clear your browser data
cs-notif-wRemembers how wide you made the notifications panel in the demoPreferenceYou clear your browser data

We do not use analytics, advertising, social media or cross-site tracking cookies, so there is nothing to opt out of.

3. Third-party services

To load the site, your browser connects to a few services. They don't set cookies through our site, but like any web server they receive your IP address and browser details when your browser requests a file from them:

  • Vercel hosts the website and keeps standard server logs for security and reliability.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typeface used on the site. See Google's privacy policy.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) supplies the spreadsheet library, loaded only when you export an Excel report from the demo.

4. The Campaign Span app

When you sign in to a Campaign Span account, we use strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in and to protect your account, for example against cross-site request forgery. The app doesn't work without them, so they can't be turned off. We will update this page with their names before accounts launch.

5. Your choices

You can clear or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings at any time. If you do, the site still works. It will just forget your light or dark mode choice. Your browser's help pages explain how:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

If we ever add analytics or other non-essential cookies, we will ask for your consent first and update this policy.
