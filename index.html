Inside the app

One workspace from idea to insight

Every piece of content lives on the calendar, moves through the board, ships through production, and comes back as data.

Content calendar

A calendar built for daily use, not quarterly planning decks

Social posts and email campaigns share one fully customizable calendar. Drag to reschedule, filter by channel or department, and see at a glance where your schedule is thin.

  • Social and email marketing plans on the same timeline
  • Every entry requires a tagged person or department, so nothing is ownerless
  • Custom views, colors, fields and filters per team
Analytics & recommendations

Know what's working — and what's missing

Connect your channels and Campaign Span tracks how your schedule performs over time. It spots gaps in your calendar and recommends changes to timing, mix and frequency.

  • Performance by channel, format, weekday and campaign
  • Gap detection for empty days, quiet channels and stalled work
  • Plain-language recommendations you can apply in one click
Project boards

Kanban boards that stay in sync with the calendar

Track every piece from idea to published. Move a card and its calendar entry updates. Assign tasks, set deadlines, and the right people hear about every change.

  • Assign tasks to people and tag whole departments
  • Instant notifications for new projects and deadline changes
  • Alerts by app, email and Slack, by department role
Production hub

Run the whole shoot, not just the post

Build a full production project with the brief, goals and logistics, then add the shot schedule, crew call times and equipment list. Project files live right next to the work.

  • Project briefs, goals, logistics and deliverables
  • Production schedules and equipment check-out lists
  • A dedicated home for project files and approved assets
Reporting

Stakeholder-ready reports in one click

Export fully formatted Excel workbooks or clean CSV files with summaries, channel breakdowns and the full post log. No more copy-pasting from five dashboards.

  • Formatted .xlsx with summary, channel and post sheets
  • CSV for BI tools and spreadsheets
  • Filter by date range, campaign, channel or department
