We want Campaign Span to be usable by everyone, and we are guided by the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA) and the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. Our site supports keyboard navigation, screen readers, light and dark modes, and reduced motion settings.

If you have trouble using any part of the site or the Service, or need information in another format, email hello@campaignspan.com. We'll work with you to provide what you need, and use your feedback to improve.

6. Reporting security issues

If you believe you've found a security vulnerability, please email hello@campaignspan.com with "Security" in the subject line and enough detail for us to reproduce it. Please give us reasonable time to fix the issue before telling anyone else, and don't access or change other people's data while testing. We won't take legal action against anyone who reports an issue to us in good faith under these guidelines.

7. Copyright complaints
