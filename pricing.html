Questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every paid plan starts with a 14-day free trial with all features enabled. You can also try the demo without creating an account.

Who counts as a user?

Anyone who logs in to plan, edit or approve content. View-only stakeholders who only receive exported reports don't need a seat.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade any time, and we'll prorate the difference.

Do the mobile apps cost extra?

No. The iOS and Android apps are included with every plan.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education?

Yes, contact our team for nonprofit and education pricing.
