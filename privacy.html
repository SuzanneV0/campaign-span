1. Who we are

Campaign Span ("Campaign Span", "we", "us") operates the website at campaignspan.com and the Campaign Span content planning service (the "Service"). We are based in Ontario, Canada, and handle personal information in line with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and other applicable laws.

This policy covers our website, the demo, and the Service. Where you use the Service on behalf of an organization (for example, your employer), that organization controls the content in its workspace, and we process that content on its behalf.

2. What we collect

When you visit our website

  • Technical information that browsers send automatically, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested. Our hosting provider records this in server logs for security and reliability.
  • Preferences stored on your device, such as light or dark mode. These stay in your browser and are not sent to us. See our cookie policy.

We do not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics on our website.

When you use the demo

The demo runs entirely in your browser. Calendar entries, uploads and settings you create in the demo are not sent to us, and they disappear when you reload the page. Custom demo links contain only the settings our team chose for that demo, such as a company name and sample team names.

When you join the waitlist

If you join our early-access waitlist, we collect your name, work email, and, if you choose to give them, your company, department and team size. We use this only to tell you about early access and to plan for the teams who are waiting. Our team can see the list, and it is stored with our database provider. To be removed, email hello@campaignspan.com.

When you contact us

If you email us, we collect your name, email address and anything you choose to include, so we can reply.

When you have a Campaign Span account

  • Account information: name, work email, company, department role and password (stored only in hashed form).
  • Workspace content: calendar entries, briefs, production schedules, equipment lists, files, comments and tags that you and your team add.
  • Connected account data: when you connect a social media, email marketing, CRM or design platform, we receive the information that platform shares under the permissions you approve, such as post performance metrics, campaign results and account names. We request only the access the Service needs.
  • Notification settings: such as your email address for alerts and the Slack workspace and channels you connect.
  • Billing information: processed by our payment provider. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Usage information: such as features used, errors encountered and device type, so we can keep the Service reliable and improve it.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the Service: show your calendar, run analytics and recommendations, send the notifications you set up, and generate reports and exports.
  • To support you and respond to your questions.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To improve the Service, using aggregated or de-identified information where possible.
  • To bill for paid plans and meet our legal and accounting obligations.
  • To send product updates and marketing, only where you have agreed (see Marketing email).

We rely on your consent, which may be implied where the use is obvious from the context (for example, using your email to send notifications you turned on). You can withdraw consent at any time, subject to legal or contractual limits. Withdrawing may mean we can't provide parts of the Service.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use workspace content to target advertising.

4. Who we share it with

  • Service providers who help us run the Service, such as website and application hosting, email delivery, payment processing and customer support tools. They may use personal information only to provide services to us.
  • Platforms you connect. When you schedule or sync content, we send that platform the information needed to do so, under that platform's own terms and privacy policy.
  • Your organization. Workspace administrators can see content and activity in their workspace.
  • Legal and safety reasons, where required by law or to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, the public or Campaign Span.
  • Business transfers. If Campaign Span is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, personal information may be transferred under confidentiality obligations, and we will tell you if that happens.

5. Where it's stored

Some of our service providers store or process information outside Canada, including in the United States. While it is outside Canada, information is subject to the laws of that country and may be accessible to its courts, law enforcement and national security authorities. We use contracts and other safeguards to protect it.

6. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above. Waitlist details are kept until we invite you to create an account, or until you ask us to remove them. Workspace content is kept while your account is active. After an account is closed, we delete or de-identify its content within 90 days, except where we must keep records longer for legal, tax or accounting reasons. Backups are overwritten on a rolling schedule.

7. Security

We protect personal information with safeguards suited to its sensitivity, including encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls and limiting access to staff who need it. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify affected people and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as required by law.

8. Your rights

You can ask to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • withdraw consent or ask us to delete your information, subject to legal limits; and
  • learn how your information has been used and shared.

Email hello@campaignspan.com and we will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity first. If your request concerns workspace content controlled by your organization, we may refer you to its administrator.

If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

9. Marketing email

We send marketing email only with your consent, as required by Canada's anti-spam legislation (CASL). Every marketing email includes our contact information and an unsubscribe link. Service messages, such as security notices, billing receipts and the notifications you set up, are not marketing and continue while you have an account.

10. Children

Campaign Span is a business tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service changes. We will post the new version here with a new "Last updated" date, and tell account holders about significant changes by email or in the app before they take effect.
